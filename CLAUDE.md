## How we win (in priority order)
1. **A real pain for a named buyer.** Evidence beats opinion: quotes, prices people pay today, workarounds.
2. **A working demo** that does one thing end to end. One sharp flow beats five half-features.
3. **Evidence by judging time:** a live site with honest signups, sourced public evidence from the hypothesis screen, and any disclosed community replies. Never call it "validated".
4. **The theme link in one sentence.** Show how the product serves the event's theme, from the brief above.

## GTM standard
- Define the ICP and the wedge before any funnel. Name the exact first buyer, not "small businesses."
- Price to willingness to pay. Anchor on what the buyer spends today on the workaround, and on the cost of inaction.
- Prefer a product that owns a network (each user brings the next one) over one that is a node in someone else's.

## Working rules
- Every factual claim in `research/` carries a source URL. Unsourced means unknown.
- Timeboxes in the graphs are hard. When one expires, pick the best option so far and move on.
- Do not commit secrets. `.env` is in `.gitignore`.
- Follow your event's rules on pre-existing code. If they are unclear, ask the organizers in writing.
- Cut scope before cutting the demo. At the feature freeze time above, freeze features and polish the one flow.
