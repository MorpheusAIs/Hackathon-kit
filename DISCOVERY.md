# Discovery first: is this worth building?

A product is not complete without three maps: **product-market fit**, the **pain**, and the **cost of not solving it**. A demo that works but solves nothing is a toy. Before you write product code, show that someone has a real problem, that the problem costs them something, and that your build gives them enough value to choose it.

Value can be economic (money saved or earned) or not (time back, safety, access, dignity, trust). Either is fine. You have to name which one, and show how you would measure it.

## The four risks

This kit follows the product-discovery approach of Marty Cagan and the Silicon Valley Product Group. Every product idea carries four risks. Discovery means reducing them cheaply **before** delivery.

| Risk | The question | Cheapest hackathon test |
|---|---|---|
| **Value** | Will the buyer choose this? | Verbatim pain quotes, what they pay today, an honest signup page |
| **Usability** | Can they use it without help? | A stranger completes the one flow without you explaining it |
| **Feasibility** | Can we build it with this time, skill and tech? | One real input through the flow on Morpheus inference, early on build day |
| **Viability** | Does it work as a business, or as a sustained effort? | Who pays, the inference cost per use against the price, who keeps it running after the event |

Expect many ideas to fail one of these four. Finding that out on build day morning is a win, not a loss.

## The maps

| Map | Answer in one line, with a source | Kit skill |
|---|---|---|
| **Pain** | Who hurts, in their own words, how often, how badly | `/market-scan`, `/pain-map` |
| **Cost of inaction** | What the pain costs each month if nothing changes: money, hours, risk, or harm | `/pain-map`, `/gtm-wedge` |
| **Product-market fit** | The exact first buyer, the one job you do better than today's workaround, and the evidence they want it | `/gtm-wedge` |
| **Value** | What the buyer gains, economic or otherwise, and how you would measure it | `/opportunity` |
| **Viability** | Who pays, the price and the unit cost, and why it can last | `/gtm-wedge`, `/opportunity` |

## The order of work

```
/market-scan (1–3 spaces) → /pain-map → /gtm-wedge → /opportunity (GO / PIVOT / STOP) → /scaffold → /poc
```

`/opportunity` is the gate. It rates the four risks with evidence. Only a **GO** moves on to building. A **PIVOT** sends you back to the runner-up pain. A **STOP** is cheaper today than at the finals.

## Rules of evidence

1. Quote, don't summarize. Each claim gets a URL, a date and a verbatim line.
2. Count independent people, not posts.
3. Look for counter-evidence: happy users of the current workaround, "already solved" threads.
4. Call the result a **hypothesis screen**. Signups show interest, not payment. Do not write "validated".
