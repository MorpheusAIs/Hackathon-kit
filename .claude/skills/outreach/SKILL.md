# Outreach agent (approval-gated)

Key insight: **the quote is the lead.** Businesses that reply to a quote or sample request are already in conversation with you, so the pitch feels natural instead of cold.

## Step 1: Lead sourcing (filters are hard gates)
Search Google Maps, directories, trade associations or LinkedIn for <NICHE> in <CITY/SEGMENT>. Keep a lead only if **all** of these hold (adapt the filters to the niche, and write them down first):
- Established: 4+ stars and 50+ reviews, or a clear sign of a real operating business.
- Visible gap your product fixes, e.g. no booking link, owner never answers reviews, slow or no reply to the contact form, no online quoting.
Log each lead with its source URL.

## Step 2: Audit (one page each)
Score 1–10 on the dimensions your product improves (response time, booking or quoting process, reviews, website speed, listing gaps). Add the top 3 fixes and an estimate of the revenue at stake, **labeled as an estimate**.

## Step 3: Message and demo
- Draft a short, plain message in your own voice: who you are and that you are building at <event>, the one gap you found, and a link to a demo made for them (their name or logo, their gap).
- List every public way to reach the owner.

## Output: `research/outreach.md`
A ranked table (lead, score, gap, audit link, draft message, channel), with the 9 best at the top.

## Hard rules
- **Never contact anyone without explicit approval** of the exact message and recipient list, from the approver named in CLAUDE.md. The agent drafts; a human sends.
- Email (US CAN-SPAM): real sender name, real reply address, a valid postal address, a clear note that the message is a business pitch, and an opt-out that you honor within 10 business days. Keep a do-not-contact list.
- Store prospect details in `research/outreach.md` only while you need them. Do not quote or screenshot a prospect publicly without their written consent.
- Low volume (tens per day, not hundreds), sent from a real inbox.
- **No automated calls or texts** to phone numbers (US TCPA law). Manual calls only.
- Do not submit fake requests or misrepresent yourselves. If you ask for a quote, either be a real potential buyer, or say up front that it's for a demo.
- Do not scrape behind logins or ignore a site's terms.
